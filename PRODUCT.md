# SkyLink UAV Training Platform

## Overview
SkyLink is a professional online learning platform for students and engineers specializing in UAV (unmanned aerial vehicle) technology. The platform provides comprehensive training from foundational control theory to advanced operational practices.

## Users
- **Students**: University-level learners entering the UAV/aerospace field
- **Engineers**: Professional technicians and system engineers upgrading skills
- **Educators**: Instructors managing courses and student progress
- **Researchers**: Scientists conducting UAV-related studies

## Brand Purpose
Democratize access to professional-grade UAV education through structured, industry-aligned curriculum with hands-on experience.

## Brand Tone
- **Professional yet approachable**: Technical rigor without intimidation
- **Trustworthy and authoritative**: Industry standards, not DIY
- **Forward-thinking**: Innovation and cutting-edge technology
- **Practical**: Real equipment, real applications, real results

## Key Value Propositions
1. Industry-aligned curriculum (Matrice 300 RTK, Autel Dragonfish, Skydio 2+)
2. Hands-on laboratory equipment and real-world scenarios
3. Comprehensive knowledge progression (basics → advanced specialization)
4. Expert instruction from practicing engineers and researchers

## Register
Product (app UI focused on learning and discovery, not marketing campaign)

## Anti-references
- Flat, generic SaaS landing pages (Notion, Figma clones)
- Overly playful consumer education sites
- Dark-first tech maximalism
- Crypto/AI startup aesthetics

## Design Principles
1. **Clarity over decoration**: Every visual element serves navigation or understanding
2. **Professional minimalism**: Clean layouts, generous whitespace, thoughtful hierarchy
3. **Purposeful color**: One primary brand color (blue for tech/aviation), accents for categorization
4. **Technical precision**: Crisp typography, exact measurements, no approximations
5. **Motion with intent**: Smooth transitions that aid comprehension, never distract

## Success Criteria
- Users immediately understand: what is SkyLink, what can I learn, how to get started
- Professional appearance inspires confidence in curriculum quality
- Clear visual separation between course categories and platforms
- Responsive design works perfectly on desktop, tablet, and mobile
- Loading performance optimized for diverse network conditions
